Our Story

Shoe designer Marcus Vance started Sole & Stride Footwear in 2025 to give customers comfortable, modern, and strong shoes. What began as a small shop selling sneakers and leather boots grew rapidly thanks to exceptional customer satisfaction and word-of-mouth trust.

Mission & Vision

Mission Statement

To make comfortable, stylish, and long-lasting shoes that give customers confidence every day.

Vision Statement

To become a top shoe brand around the world by creating great designs and building strong trust with our customers.

Who We Serve

  • Young Professionals & Students (Ages 18–30): Looking for trendy and affordable daily sneakers.
  • Active People & Athletes (Ages 20–45): Seeking durable running and training shoes.
  • Corporate Workers & Event Guests (Ages 25–60): Wanting premium formal dress shoes.

Materials & Sustainability

We source skin-safe, eco-friendly materials and ethical leather options to ensure every pair minimizes environmental impact while maximizing foot support and lifespan.
